Even a well-planned project needs consistent oversight once work is underway. We visit site on a regular schedule to check that what is being built matches the approved drawings, specification and material samples.

Each visit is documented with photos and notes, so you have a clear, dated record of progress and any issues raised with the contractor, along with how they were resolved.

This service works alongside a contractor of your choice or as part of our turnkey execution, giving you an independent set of eyes on quality throughout construction.
