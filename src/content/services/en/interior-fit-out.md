Fit-out is where a design becomes a physical space. We manage the construction works needed to take a unit from shell condition, or from its existing state, to the finished interior — partitions, flooring, ceilings, joinery and wall finishes, coordinated on site day to day.

Custom joinery and millwork are produced to match the approved drawings and material samples, and mechanical, electrical and plumbing works are coordinated with the relevant trades so nothing is left disconnected from the design.

Every stage is checked against the design and specification before we move to the next, so issues are caught early rather than at handover.
