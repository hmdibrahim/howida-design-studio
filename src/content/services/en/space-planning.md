Good space planning is felt more than it is seen — it is the reason a home or office feels easy to move through instead of cramped or awkward. We start with accurate site measurements and an honest look at how each area will actually be used.

We test multiple layout options against real furniture dimensions, circulation paths and storage needs before settling on a plan, so the choice you approve has already been stress-tested against daily life.

This service is often the foundation for a larger interior design or fit-out project, but it also stands on its own for clients who need a clear, buildable layout plan.
