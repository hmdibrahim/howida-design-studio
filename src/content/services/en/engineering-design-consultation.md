Interior design decisions often run into structural, electrical or mechanical constraints that only surface once an engineer looks closely. We bring that review in early, so layout and design choices are checked for feasibility before they reach site.

This includes reviewing structural and MEP implications of proposed changes, supporting the documentation needed for authority approvals, and coordinating with specialist consultants where a project requires them.

The goal is straightforward: fewer surprises during execution, and a design that was engineered to work from the start, not adjusted after the fact.
